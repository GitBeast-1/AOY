# Art OF YouTube (AOY) - Chrome Extension

1-Click capture of YouTube video thumbnails, titles, channel details, and live metrics directly into your AOY Faceless Studio workspace.

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (15 Seconds)

1. **Download & Extract**:
   - Download `aoy-youtube-extension.zip`
   - Extract/unzip the contents to a folder on your computer.

2. **Open Extensions in Chrome**:
   - In Google Chrome, navigate to: `chrome://extensions`
   - Turn **ON** the **"Developer mode"** toggle in the top-right corner.

3. **Load the Extension**:
   - Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left.
   - Select the extracted `extension` folder containing `manifest.json`.

4. **Start Capturing**:
   - Go to any video on [YouTube.com](https://www.youtube.com).
   - Look right below the video title and subscribe bar.
   - You will see the amber button: **⚡ Copy to AOY Thumbs**.
   - Click it once: it copies high-res thumbnail data, channel details, and video stats to your clipboard AND immediately syncs with your AOY Studio app!
